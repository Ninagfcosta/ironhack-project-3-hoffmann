# Client Briefs — Shared with Partner



#Case 1

A big store chain wants to use cameras that are already inside the shops to watch people's faces while they shop. The AI would try to guess how the person is feeling (stressed, excited, sad) just by looking at their face and body. If the AI thinks someone is close to buying something on impulse, a discount pops up on a nearby screen right away. Shoppers have no idea this is happening. Nobody checks what the AI does before the discount shows up.

#Case 2

A company that finds people jobs gets tons of resumes every day, so they want an AI to read all of them and put them in order, best to worst, based on things like years of experience and keywords. A real person only looks at the top 10 resumes before calling anyone for an interview. Everyone below that gets an automatic "no," and no human ever looks at those.

#Case 3

An online store that sells electronics wants a chat robot on their website and app to answer easy questions like "where's my order" or "how do I return this," 24 hours a day. If the question is too hard, it passes the conversation to a real human.

#Case 4

A bakery chain wants a simple AI that looks at last week's sales and tells them how many breads and cakes to bake tomorrow, so they don't waste food. The store manager can always change the AI's suggestion before actually ordering the ingredients.
